Contemporary cartoon with retro mascot energy.

Thick black outlines, even and firm, around every single shape. Colour is
clean saturated flat fill — no gradients, no rendering. Forms are massive and
extremely readable even at small size, with proportions pushed a little past
life. Text is bold cartoon lettering, outlined in black like everything else.
The background stays light.

Avoid: 3D, volume, computed shadows, texture, grain, thin or hesitant line
work.
