Warm contemporary pixel art, in the language of narrative indie games.

Wide, clearly visible square pixels on a firm grid. Jagged edges everywhere,
no anti-aliasing, no blur, no smooth curves; diagonals step. Effective
resolution is low, as if the image had been drawn on a small grid and scaled
up by nearest neighbour.

A tight palette — around thirty colours for the whole image — warm and
slightly muted: cream, warm oak, terracotta, dusty coral, dulled cobalt, sage
and forest green, mustard, soft pink, charcoal. Flat fills, no gradients.
Shading is restrained, two or three tones per material, plus ordered
checkerboard dithering where a transition is needed. Ambient light is warm and
soft, never contrasty.

People are human, warm and readable: very simple faces, eyes one or two pixels
wide, a small mouth, features suggested rather than drawn, proportions pushed a
little with larger heads and hands. The mood is lived-in contemporary daily
life, with small narrative details in the set.

Avoid: smooth vector look, anti-aliased edges, airbrush or blurred shading,
photorealism, 3D, gradients, high-resolution detail, tiny illegible pixels,
more than about thirty colours.
