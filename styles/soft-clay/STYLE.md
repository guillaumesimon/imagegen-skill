Stylised 3D characters moulded out of matte clay, in the register of a
contemporary mobile game hero render.

Everything reads as soft plasticine: rounded edges, no sharp corners anywhere,
and a faintly uneven surface with thumbprint dimples catching the light.
Proportions are exaggerated and toy-like — oversized head, compact body, chunky
hands and feet, a big silhouette that stays readable at thumbnail size.

Bright saturated colours on simple large forms. Studio lighting: a soft key from
the upper left, a warm bounce fill, and a bright rim light peeling the subject
off the background. Shading is soft and waxy, with a hint of light scattering
through the thinner parts. Plain gradient backdrop, gentle depth of field.

Avoid: hard surfaces, metal and glass, realistic human proportions, flat 2D
shading, cluttered backgrounds.
