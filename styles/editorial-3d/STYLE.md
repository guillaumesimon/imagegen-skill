Playful editorial 3D in matte materials. Tangible, premium, expressive,
contemporary — the register of a magazine opener rendered in clay.

Everything is sculpted from smooth matte clay: no specular highlights, no
realistic surface texture, no photographic detail. Corners are rounded
everywhere and there are no sharp edges. Forms are simplified and iconic, with
slightly chunky, almost toy-like proportions, but the finish is refined rather
than childish. Light is soft and diffuse, arriving from the upper left, with
tender contact shadows and low contrast. The background is light, cream or
white.

People have simplified features, dot eyes, no skin texture.

Avoid: photorealism, product-photo look, realistically textured wood or fruit,
leather, brushed metal, glossy reflections.

Production notes. That Avoid list is not optional. Leave it out and the model
slides into photographic rendering — especially on scenes with no character in
them, where nothing else is pulling it toward illustration.
