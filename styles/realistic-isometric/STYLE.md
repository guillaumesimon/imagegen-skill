Semi-realistic isometric miniature. Detailed, tangible, premium — a scale
model of the scene.

Strict isometric projection, three-quarter view at thirty degrees, looking
down. The scene sits on a square slab with four right-angled corners and a
visible edge thickness, standing on a plain studio backdrop. Nothing holds the
slab up: no legs, no furniture underneath.

Materials are credible and varied: real wood grain, glazed ceramic, brushed
brass, frosted glass, woven linen, terracotta, paper. Warm soft studio light,
realistic shadows, ambient occlusion. A lot of small believable detail
throughout, and the subject fills the frame. A person can be present, but as
one element of the space rather than the subject of the image.

Avoid: front view, flat top-down view, vector look, cartoon outlines, matte
clay, toy plastic, unbalanced composition.

Production notes. The camera is never inferred from a reference image — write
it into the prompt every time. Same for the slab standing on nothing, and for
the subject filling the frame. Leave either out and the model puts a wobbly
stool under the slab, or drops a tiny subject into a sea of empty space.
