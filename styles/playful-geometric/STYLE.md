Modern shapes, human stories. Flat geometric illustration in a confident
poster register — balanced, bold, timeless.

Everything is built out of geometric primitives: circles, arcs, rounded
rectangles, straight lines. Objects are reduced to the most abstract symbol
that still reads. No outlines anywhere — forms are defined by colour alone, in
strict flat fills, usually only three or four dominant colours across the whole
image. Palette: cream ground, cobalt blue, coral red, leaf green, mustard
yellow, powder pink, terracotta, charcoal. The background is always light,
cream or white, never dark.

No volume of any kind: no drop shadows, no gradients, no perspective. People
are radically simplified — one solid shape for the hair, dot eyes, no nose.
Text sits flush in the flat plane, set clean and geometric. The composition
stays calm and generous, with a lot of empty space around the subject.

Avoid: 3D, volume, drop shadows, texture, outlines, clutter, dark backgrounds.
