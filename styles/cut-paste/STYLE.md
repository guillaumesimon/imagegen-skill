Cut paper and layered collage. Tactile, graphic, contemporary editorial.

Read it as a flat photograph of a scene built entirely from real stacked
paper. Every element is a piece of paper, scissor-cut or hand-torn, with
slightly irregular edges and visible paper fibre. Each layer casts a soft
physical shadow on the one beneath it, which is what gives the image its
depth. A few pieces of masking tape hold things in place.

Shapes stay graphic and simplified, in a contemporary editorial register —
this is paper as an illustration medium, not a craft project.

Avoid: 3D clay rendering, smooth digital gradients, airbrush shading, flat
vector look, childish craft aesthetic.
