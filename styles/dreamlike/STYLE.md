Editorial illustration running on dream logic — the kind of full-page image that
sits alongside a long-form magazine piece.

Pastel palette: powder pink, pale sky blue, soft lilac, warm cream, muted sage.
The colours sit close together in value and nothing goes truly dark. Broad flat
shapes with soft airbrushed gradients inside them, and a fine paper grain laid
over the whole image.

Light is diffuse and arrives from everywhere at once. Shadows are soft, long,
and coloured rather than grey. Scale is unreliable on purpose: objects float, a
horizon curves, a staircase leads somewhere it shouldn't. The composition stays
calm and generous, with a lot of empty space around the subject.

Avoid: harsh contrast, black outlines, photographic realism, busy detail,
saturated primaries.
