Risograph print with texture, colour and good energy. Limited inks, grain and
the character of something actually printed.

Four inks only — fluorescent pink, bright blue, sunflower yellow, coral red —
on textured cream paper. Grain and paper tooth are strongly visible and the
halftone screen shows. The inks overprint and mix into new colours where they
cross. Registration is deliberately off: flat areas sit a millimetre away from
their own outlines, and ink coverage is uneven across a fill.

Shapes stay flat and simplified, with no gradients and no rendered shading.
Text is hand-drawn lettering printed in a single solid ink so it survives the
grain.

Avoid: crisp vector edges, digital smoothness, 3D volume, photorealism,
illegible text.
