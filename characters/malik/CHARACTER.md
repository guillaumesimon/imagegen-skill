Malik is a Black man in his late twenties, tall and lean, with deep brown skin,
a square jaw and dark brown eyes. Open, easy expression; he tends to look
slightly off-camera rather than straight down the lens.

His hair is cut short with a sharp fade at the temples and a single razor part
traced on the left side. The beard is close-cropped and squared off along the
jaw. A small silver hoop hangs in his left ear.

He wears a cobalt blue cotton chore jacket over a plain white tee, worn open or
buttoned.
That jacket colour is the thing that carries his identity across styles when the
face gets simplified away.

Note: his sheet is photographic and `editorial-photo` is not a style in the
library — same for Inès and `manga`. Only Momo's `megadrive` has a matching
entry. That is on purpose: it exercises both paths, a character whose native
style is on the shelf and characters whose native style only exists on the sheet.
