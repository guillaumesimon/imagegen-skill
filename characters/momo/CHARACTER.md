Momo is a young French bulldog, small and stocky, with the breed's flat muzzle,
wide-set round eyes and heavy shoulders. The head is large for the body, the
chest broad, the legs short and thick. His expression sits between alert and
faintly indignant: ears up, brow wrinkled, mouth a little open.

Short smooth fawn coat, darkening to a near-black mask across the muzzle and
around the eyes. A single white teardrop patch on the chest, no other markings.
Red webbing collar with a round brass tag hanging at the throat.

The mismatched ears are the most recognisable thing about him — right one
straight, left one tipped forward — and they survive every level of stylisation,
down to a 16-pixel sprite. Keep them in every render.
