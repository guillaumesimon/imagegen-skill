Inès is a woman in her late twenties, slim and of average height, with an oval
face, fair skin and dark brown eyes. Her expression is calm and a little wry —
she looks like she is about to disagree politely.

Her hair is dark brown, cut in a blunt bob that stops level with the jawline,
with a straight fringe sitting just above the eyebrows. The silhouette is what
reads first at any size: a clean rectangle framing the face.

She wears round glasses with thin gold wire frames, a cream ribbed turtleneck,
and an oversized washed-denim jacket with the sleeves pushed up to the forearm.
A small mole sits under her right eye.
